**One Image: Saturday 22 November 2026.** A single 1024×1024 PNG that sums up the day's world news as a newspaper front page: Russian missiles hitting Polish soil as the lead, Iran's strike on Bahrain beneath it, six sections (World · US & Politics · Business & Tech · Science & Health · Sport · Culture & Odds), a dateline rail, the day in numbers, and eight world briefs — 28 stories in one image, drawn from live wire reports filed 21–22 Nov 2026 (Reuters, AP, BBC, Al Jazeera, Washington Post, Bloomberg, The Guardian and more). `headlines.json` holds the stories, `render.py` draws the page deterministically, `image_manifest.json` records the artifact's hash, and `main.ts` verifies the PNG against both.

## Run it

```sh
python3 render.py && node main.ts
```
